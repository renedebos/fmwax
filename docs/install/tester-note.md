# A note for testers

*To send with the link to https://github.com/renedebos/fmwax.*

---

Thanks for trying fmwax. It puts radio-style DJs on your WiiM's preset buttons, curated by Claude or ChatGPT. You ask a DJ for music on your phone (*"Hank, forty minutes of 45s"*), and a few minutes later the set is ready on a WiiM preset button, or playing if you asked it to play. A press of that preset plays it again. With the optional *Endless* part, a DJ keeps playing for as long as you listen.

**What you need**

- A WiiM speaker, set up in the WiiM Home app.
- Spotify Premium.
- Claude Pro (or higher), or ChatGPT Plus (or higher). Nobody has tried the free plans yet: if you're on one, please try anyway and tell me how far you get.
- A free Cloudflare account, and a free GitHub account if you use the Deploy button. (Start here also gives a way without GitHub.)
- A computer with a web browser.
- About an hour, plus 30 to 45 minutes if you add Endless. Most of the time is saving presets on the WiiM.

**How:** open the link above and follow **Start here** from the top. You make up a password, press the Deploy button, then follow a checklist on your house's setup page. (No GitHub account? Start here links to a way without it.)

**Once it plays:** three DJs take any ask. **Genre** for a style (*"Genre, an hour of ska"*), **Deep Dive** for an artist (*"Deep Dive, forty minutes of early Prince"*), and **the Librarian** for a subject (*"Librarian, an introduction to Stax"*), who also writes notes to read, kept on your house's Shelf. Then try the others: each has their own taste.

**Good to know**

- Everything runs on *your* accounts: your Cloudflare, your Spotify, your Claude or ChatGPT. I can't see your house or your music.
- The home helper is optional, any time later: a small program on a computer at home, so your house can see and press every preset.
- Not in this version: Tidal, and the Talk tab (it needs an OpenAI key). The Claude and ChatGPT apps' own voice modes work.
- Updates: your setup page says when one is out. When an update's note says **re-add the connector**, do that too (in ChatGPT, rescan the plugin). The Updates page in the repository explains both.

**What to tell me**, in a direct message to **Rene** on the WiiM forum (forum.wiimhome.com):

- Where you got stuck or unsure, and which step it was.
- A screenshot of what you saw. Crop out your house's address and your recovery code: either one lets someone run your house.
- What you expected, and what happened instead.
- Your phone and computer (for example: iPhone, Mac).
- Your house's version: the last line of the House rules page (Speakers, then House rules).

Small things count: a word that confused you is as useful as a step that failed.

Rene

# DJ presets (Level 2)

A DJ on a preset button of their own. Add one once the steps in [Start here](../../START-HERE.md) work. It needs nothing more than Level 1, and takes a few minutes per added preset.

## What you get

- **A preset that is that DJ's.** Press it and they play. It comes with about an hour of their songs, so you can save it on your WiiM right away.
- **New songs when you ask.** Ask that DJ for a set in Claude or ChatGPT, for example *"Hank, an hour for tonight"*. It lands on their own preset, in place of the songs there. Your Requests preset stays as it was.
- **It plays its songs and stops.** Nothing adds songs by itself at this level. That is [Endless DJs](endless.md), Level 3.

Seven DJs can have a preset of their own: Dr. Funkenstein, Hank, Vibe, Americana, Soul, Pub Rock and Sessions. Deep Dive and Genre need telling what to play each time, and the Librarian and Gatefold write programs: ask those four for a set and it goes on Requests.

## Set it up

On the setup page, under **Level 2 · DJ presets**:

1. Click **Add a DJ preset**.
2. Choose the DJ and a free preset number. Pick a number that holds nothing you keep on your WiiM, such as a radio station.
3. Your house makes a playlist named for the DJ in your Spotify account. Save it on that preset of each WiiM, as the page shows: play it from the Spotify app, then in WiiM Home tap the song bar, **…**, **Preset**, and the number.

**Start with one.** Each preset takes a few minutes to save on every WiiM, and three is a good number to stop at.

## Good to know

- **Remove**, on the same page, frees the number. The playlist stays in your Spotify, and your WiiM keeps playing it on that preset until you save something else there.
- **The playlist is empty, or won't play:** ask the DJ for a set first. It fills the playlist. Then save the preset.
- **Later, with Endless,** the same presets add songs by themselves while they play. From then on, a set you ask a DJ for goes on Requests.

Start your fmwax house

Three steps on this page, about ten minutes; your house's setup page takes it from there. Keep this page open until the last one. First time here? Read Start here for what you need: a WiiM, Spotify Premium, Claude or ChatGPT, a free Cloudflare account and a free GitHub account.

What you are starting

Today you install Level 1. The rest you add later, from your house's setup page, when you want it. You can stop at any level and it works.

Level 1 · todayAsk a DJ Ask for a set in Claude or ChatGPT. It plays on your WiiM and stays on one preset. About an hour
Level 2 · laterDJ presets A DJ on a button of their own, with an hour of their songs from the start. Needs nothing more
Level 3 · laterEndless Your DJ presets add songs by themselves for as long as you listen. Needs Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus

Extras, at any level: the home helper, on a computer at home, and Talk, voice with your own OpenAI key. What each level needs

1Your setup key

This is your house's password for setting it up. It was made in this browser just now, and nobody else has it.

…

This browser remembers the key, so you can come back to this page. Paste it into a note of your own too, in case this browser forgets it before you are done. Once your house is claimed, the key has done its work.

2Deploy to Cloudflare

The button below opens Cloudflare in a new tab. Keep this tab open beside it: you come back here for each step, and once to press the button a second time.

Signed in to other Cloudflare or GitHub accounts in this browser? Then open this page in a private window first, so your house can't land on the wrong account. (The key is made again there: use that one.)

  1. Press the button, and sign in to Cloudflare. A form called Set up your application opens.
    Deploy to Cloudflare
  2. Connect GitHub. On the form, click the Git account box to open its list.
    • Your GitHub name is in the list: choose it, and skip to Change four things on the form, below.
    • It is not: choose New GitHub connection, sign in to GitHub, choose All repositories, then click Install & Authorize.
    • GitHub shows a settings page with a greyed-out Save button, and no Install: Cloudflare is already connected to your GitHub. Change nothing there, and go on to the next step.
  3. Cloudflare may now show "Select a repository". Don't pick anything there. Press the button again, here. The form opens once more: click the Git account box and choose your GitHub name from the list. The rest of the form then appears.
    Deploy to Cloudflare, again
  4. Change four things on the form, and leave the rest. The form scrolls inside its own box.
    Create private Git repositoryCheck this box.
    The two Select KV namespace boxesIn each, choose + Create new. Name the first fmwax-house-tokens and the second fmwax-house-programs.
    SETUP_KEYPaste your key from step 1.
    Enable Preview buildsNear the bottom. Switch it off if it is on.
  5. Click Deploy and wait about a minute, until the log ends with Success! Build completed. Then click Copy build log, at the top right of the log, and go on to 3, Open your setup page, below on this page.

Pictures of the form, and what to do if the build fails: Start here, step 2.

3Open your setup page

Paste the build log you just copied into this box: the page finds your house's address in it. Then the button opens your house's setup page with your key already filled in. There, click Claim this house.

Or paste the address alone. In the log it is the line that starts with https:// and ends with workers.dev, a few lines above Success. Cloudflare's own pages may say "No URLs enabled": the address works all the same.
